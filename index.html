About

I work across the full life of a data and AI programme, starting well before the technology. That means working with leadership on business strategy and analysis: which problems are worth solving, what the options are, what the business case looks like and what the risks are. From there I shape the programme, build the analytics, models and intelligence behind it, and run the delivery so that what gets built changes how the business operates.

My background combines software engineering, data science and quantitative finance with senior advisory work on strategy, risk and investment decisions. I've led programmes and delivery teams, managed vendors and budgets, and run the stakeholder side of change: aligning executives, winning sign-off, and keeping the people affected on board through rollout. I've spent years translating technical results into options and recommendations for boards, investors and non-technical teams. I still write the code, which means the strategy I advise on is grounded in what can be built.

What I do

  • Business strategy and analysis. Working with leadership to define the problem, appraise options, build the business case and set the measures of success. Strategic, financial and operational analysis that turns a direction into a plan.
  • AI and data programme delivery. Owning the programme end to end: scoping, stakeholder alignment, vendor and budget control, rollout, training and handover. Governance, risk and assurance built into the plan from day one, so the result stands up to a board, an auditor or a regulator.
  • Data-led decision making and business intelligence. Decision dashboards, KPI frameworks and executive reporting designed around the decision they support, and the insight work that converts analysis into recommendations leaders can act on.
  • AI implementation. LLM applications, retrieval-augmented assistants and agent workflows deployed on real organisational data, with evaluation, guardrails and monitoring in place from the first release.
  • Data science, econometrics and advanced analytics. Forecasting, causal analysis, experiment design, risk modelling and statistical modelling on financial, economic and operational data, with results that are defensible, not just predictive.
  • Forward-deployed delivery. Embedding with a business or client team to turn a loose requirement into a working system: data integration, pipelines, APIs and the tooling around them, delivered alongside the people who will run it.

The measure I care about is whether the system is still in use six months later.
