About

I work where data, AI and delivery meet. I take a problem from the first conversation to a working tool: shaping the question, building the analysis or model, and managing the rollout so the people who need it actually use it.

I explain things in plain English, keep stakeholders close throughout, and care more about what gets adopted than what looks impressive. I most enjoy working with [[charities, policy bodies, corporates and SMEs: confirm]] that want their data to change how they make decisions.
